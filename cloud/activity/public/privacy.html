EQ Legends Live

Privacy Policy

Effective August 8, 2026

EQ Legends Live is an opt-in companion view for the EQ Legends Companion desktop application. This policy explains what the cloud service processes, why, and how you can delete it.

Data we process

  • Discord identity: your Discord user ID, username, display name, and optional avatar URL, obtained with the identify OAuth scope.
  • Paired devices: a random device ID, the label shown in account controls, creation and revocation times, and a salted and peppered hash of the device secret. The service does not retain the original secret.
  • Derived EverQuest companion state: character name and ID, server, up to three detected classes, optional level and zone, a compact current-combat summary, leveling pace and ETA when available, and up to ten recent kills and loot entries.
  • Short-lived security records: hashed pairing codes, hashed single-use connection tickets, request counts, reset times, and client IP addresses embedded in short-lived rate-limit keys.
  • Infrastructure metadata: Cloudflare and Discord may process ordinary request, network, device, and diagnostic metadata while hosting and proxying the Activity.
We do not upload raw EverQuest log lines, chat, tells, guild or group messages, local file paths, machine usernames, inventory dumps, map data, settings exports, alert text, or audio.

How we use data

We use this data only to authenticate you, pair and revoke your desktop devices, relay your latest validated companion snapshot to your private Discord Activity, prevent abuse, diagnose service failures, and honor account deletion. We do not sell personal data, serve targeted advertising, or build advertising profiles.

Who receives data

Cloudflare hosts the Worker, D1 database, Durable Objects, assets, and network edge. Discord provides OAuth, the Activity iframe, and the Activity proxy, so Activity traffic may pass through Discord infrastructure. We do not intentionally disclose your data to other third parties except when required by law or necessary to protect users and the service.

Retention

  • The latest derived snapshot is deleted one hour after the last paired desktop disconnects. Encounter history is not retained.
  • The browser session cookie expires after 24 hours; no corresponding server-side session row is stored.
  • Pairing codes expire after five minutes. Connection tickets expire after 60 seconds. Both are single-use.
  • Expired pairing, ticket, and rate-limit records are removed in bounded cleanup batches scheduled every five minutes.
  • Discord account and paired-device rows remain until you choose Delete cloud account.
  • Cloudflare and Discord may retain infrastructure metadata according to their own policies and configured service settings.

Your controls

Desktop publishing is off until you pair a device and enable it. You can disable publishing, forget local credentials, revoke individual desktops from the Activity, or choose Delete cloud account. Account deletion removes all account-owned database rows, closes active room connections, and wipes the room’s stored snapshot. You may also contact us for help with access or deletion.

Cookies and security

The Activity uses a signed, 24-hour, Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=None, partitioned cookie for authentication. Connection tickets are short-lived and single-use. Payloads are bounded and reconstructed from a strict allowlist. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit the data collected and use access controls, encrypted transport, credential hashing, device revocation, and automatic expiry.

Children

The service is not directed to anyone below the minimum age required to use Discord in their country. Do not use the service if you are not permitted to use Discord.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be submitted through the EQ Legends Companion issue tracker. Avoid including private game logs, pairing codes, device secrets, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
